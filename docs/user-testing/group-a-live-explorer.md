# Group A — Try the live Structura explorer

**Time:** About 10–15 minutes  
**Who this is for:** Anyone willing to explore an unfamiliar hardware reference  
**What you need:** A web browser

We are testing the website, not your hardware knowledge. Please work without outside searches and say what you expect to happen as you go.

Do not include your name, contact details or device identifiers in your response.

## Instructions

Open **https://richfish85.github.io/structura/** and complete these tasks in order:

1. Find the Intel Celeron 300A in the 1998 material. What was it intended for?
2. Find one performance result and open the evidence behind it.
3. Find the Samsung 970 EVO 500 GB and identify its controller. Can you find the evidence connecting them?
4. In your own words, explain the difference between M.2, PCIe and NVMe.
5. Find one thing that Structura says is unresolved or not yet established.

If you cannot complete a task after about three minutes, write **stuck** and continue. That is useful feedback.

When finished, complete the [response template](response-template.md) using group `A`.
