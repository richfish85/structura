# Structura user-testing pack

Use one participant handout per person. Send the handout and [response template](response-template.md); keep this facilitator page to yourself.

## Choose a group

| Group | Best for | What exists now | Suggested participants |
|---|---|---|---:|
| [A — Live explorer](group-a-live-explorer.md) | People willing to explore an unfamiliar hardware reference | Published website | 3 |
| [B — PC pathway](group-b-pc-pathway.md) | Everyday PC owners, especially Windows 10 users | Concept only | 3–5 |
| [C — Phone pathway](group-c-phone-pathway.md) | Smartphone users who buy or borrow chargers and cables | Concept only | 3–5 |

Participants do not need technical expertise. A person may join more than one group, but record that they have seen Structura before.

## Simple facilitator rules

1. Assign a code such as `A01`; do not record a name or contact details.
2. Say: “We are testing Structura, not you. There are no trick questions.”
3. Ask the participant to follow the handout without coaching.
4. If you give a hint, record the task as assisted.
5. Capture their words before discussing possible improvements.
6. Do not ask for account details, serial numbers, IMEI numbers or screenshots containing personal information.

For Group A, stop an individual task after three minutes. Groups B and C evaluate an idea, so do not imply that Structura can already produce the proposed result.

## What to compare

Across all groups, look for:

- whether the purpose is clear;
- whether the participant can describe the expected result;
- which wording causes confusion;
- what evidence they expect;
- what they would do next;
- whether they would use or recommend the pathway.

Do not convert comments into product changes during the session. Record repeated findings and choose later work through a separate milestone.
