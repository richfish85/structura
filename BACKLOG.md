## Product and data

- **Run the real-person explorer trial.** Three sessions and the task script remain pending in `docs/usability-trial.md`. Use observed failures to choose later navigation or modelling work.
- **Define the canonical promotion procedure.** The schema has status fields and `review_events`, but the complete human approval transaction and policy for ontology promotion still need a future milestone.
- **Resolve selected historical identity/event questions.** OverDrive product identity, Xeon 450 shipment status, and other open audit findings remain visible; choose a bounded subset rather than treating all findings as one task.
- **Choose code, data, and contribution licences before accepting contributions or encouraging reuse.** The repository is publicly visible without an open-source/data licence; `NOTICE.md` records the current boundary. A future contribution milestone should also cover image rights, takedown handling, governance, and redistribution.
- **Test a lightweight incentive for usability participants.** Candidate rewards include a post-test hardware reveal card, founding-tester acknowledgement or shared map progress. Reward completion and honest confusion, not correct answers or speed.
- **Define a claim-level recognition ladder before gathering public hardware evidence.** Separate a lead, documented item, observed specimen, demonstrated function, corroborated distribution, disputed claim and canonical human decision. One item may hold different states for different claims.
- **Design the contribution loop before opening submissions.** Define source and image rights, redaction of serial/IMEI/location data, duplicate credit, independent verification, abuse handling and how accepted artifacts reach non-canonical staging.
- **Keep engagement, evidence credibility and canonical authority separate.** Visual progress, quests and contributor acknowledgement may reward participation; popularity, points and social reactions must not approve factual claims.
