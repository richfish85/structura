# 1997 GPU scout notes

## Scope

Six representative 1997 graphics identities were recorded: three chips and three add-in boards. Chip and board records are intentionally separate even where a board uses a named chip.

## Evidence limits

- NVIDIA RIVA 128 relies on a retrospective first-party chronology for April launch timing.
- S3 ViRGE/MX has sampling and planned Q2 production language, not a proven first commercial shipment.
- PERMEDIA 2 evidence supports 1997 board availability plans but not a dated initial chip shipment.
- The Canopus, Melco, and ATI board records give month or late-month availability windows rather than exact sales dates.

## Omission leads

Do not create entities from these leads without a separate scout scope: 3Dfx Voodoo Rush; ATI 3D Rage Pro chip identity; Matrox Millennium II or Mystique 220; Rendition Verite V2200; PowerVR PCX2; Number Nine workstation boards; and other evidenced mobile products.

## Validation

- Six unique candidate keys and six unique source keys.
- Every candidate source URL is present in `sources.csv`.
- No verification, normalization, audit, context research, database write, or canonical promotion occurred.
