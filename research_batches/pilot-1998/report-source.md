# Structura 1998 Launch-Cohort Pilot Research Report

Audience: project owner and future research coordinators  
Date: 2026-09-08  
Status: internal source report; non-canonical research synthesis

## Scope and assumptions

The research question is whether a small, parallel-agent workflow can produce a provenance-first 1998 hardware launch cohort across CPU, GPU, and HDD products while preserving product identity, event timing, stated purpose, comparable performance evidence, and uncertainty.

The population is bounded to the candidates admitted by the pilot scouts. A record qualifies on evidence of a new product introduction or first commercial shipment during 1998. Announcement, sampling, planned production, mass production, limited OEM shipment, and broad retail availability remain separate events. The report does not claim an exhaustive worldwide census.

## Direct answer

The workflow is viable. Thirty-four candidates completed scouting, independent verification, normalization proposal, purpose research, and audit coverage without canonical promotion. The import controls rejected three classes of malformed hand-off before they entered the review database: invented audit enums, shifted CSV fields, and a legacy surplus field. Six benchmark observations met the comparison-group standard; weaker or differently configured results were left as explicit gaps.

## CPU findings

The 22 CPU candidates cover desktop, mobile, server/workstation, and upgrade segments. Intel launch releases provide strong evidence for Basic PC, mainstream performance, mobile, workstation/server, and upgrade positioning. AMD filings support K6/K6-2 product-family and speed-roadmap context but often only at quarter precision. [Intel April 1998 launch release](https://www.intel.com/pressroom/archive/releases/1998/dp041598.htm), [Intel August 1998 launch release](https://www.intel.com/pressroom/archive/releases/1998/dp082498.htm), [AMD Q4 1998 retrospective filing](https://ir.amd.com/financial-information/sec-filings/content/0001012870-99-000143/EX-99.txt).

The strongest measured comparison is the Intel MU440EX Celeron series. SPEC published Intel-submitted SPECint95 results of 8.30, 11.3, and 12.3 and SPECfp95 results of 6.88, 9.01, and 9.50 for Celeron 300, 300A, and 333 respectively under the disclosed same-platform groups. These are vendor-sponsored standards-body results, not neutral consumer-application testing. [SPECint95 result index](https://www.spec.org/osg/cpu95/results/cint95.html), [SPECfp95 result index](https://www.spec.org/cpu95/results/cfp95.html), [Celeron 300A disclosure](https://www.spec.org/osg/cpu95/results/res98q3/cpu95-980831-03027.html), [Celeron 300 disclosure](https://www.spec.org/osg/cpu95/results/res98q3/cpu95-980831-03031.html).

The principal CPU review blockers are the likely one-product/two-output OverDrive structure, the Xeon 450 announcement-versus-shipment boundary, Xeon cache/SKU granularity, and incomplete independent corroboration for several manufacturer-controlled timing claims.

## GPU findings

Eight candidates test chip-versus-board identity across Intel, NVIDIA, Matrox, ATI, and 3Dfx. Intel740 and Intel Express 3D are explicitly different objects: a graphics accelerator chip and an Intel add-in card. NVIDIA evidence is retrospective and month-level. Matrox, ATI, and 3Dfx timing depends on contemporary technical reporting where accessible first-party releases were unavailable. [Intel740 release](https://www.intel.com/pressroom/archive/releases/1998/CS021298.HTM), [Intel Express 3D release](https://www.intel.com/pressroom/archive/releases/1998/CN041598.HTM), [NVIDIA 1998 timeline](https://www.nvidia.com/content/timeline/time_98.html), [ATI RAGE 128 contemporary report](https://pc.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/article/980828/ati.htm).

No GPU benchmark observation was retained. Available figures did not survive the requirement for materially equivalent board, driver, system, workload, metric, and result provenance. The audit retains proposed-only leads including Voodoo2, Savage3D, partner TNT/G200/ATI boards, mobile products, and professional accelerators.

## HDD findings

Four candidates test family/model boundaries, capacity/interface variants, and channel-specific timing. Quantum evidence supports Fireball EL/EX production timing; IBM-related contemporary reports distinguish limited 1998 PC-maker shipments for Deskstar 25GP/22GXP from broader Q1 1999 reseller availability. [Quantum 1998 Form 10-Q](https://investors.quantum.com/financial-information/sec-filings/content/0000950005-98-000669/0000950005-98-000669.pdf), [Quantum Fireball EX contemporary report](https://pc.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/article/980609/quantum.htm), [IBM News republication](https://esj.com/articles/1998/12/01/inside-ibm_633718632899221700.aspx), [Reuters report](https://www.wired.com/1998/11/new-ibm-hard-drive-size-matters/).

No HDD benchmark observation was retained because no shared configuration and measurement provenance was established across the frozen candidates. Omission leads include Maxtor, Seagate, Western Digital, IBM Microdrive, mobile, and enterprise/SCSI families.

## Material limitations and disagreements

- “Why it existed” generally records public positioning, not private internal motive.
- Manufacturer and SEC-hosted statements are evidence of what the company said; they are not independent outcome verification.
- Historical source survival is uneven, particularly for 1998 GPU and HDD manufacturers.
- Planned production and expected card availability are not completed shipments.
- Family, chip, board, model, speed grade, cache configuration, and orderable SKU must not be counted as interchangeable units.
- Benchmark absence is a research gap, not proof of no performance difference.

## Stop rationale

The pilot stops at human review because every bounded candidate has completed the declared research stages, material conflicts are explicit, malformed inputs have been corrected, and additional searching would primarily expand the census rather than change the pilot's workflow conclusion. Omission leads remain a separately authorized expansion backlog.
