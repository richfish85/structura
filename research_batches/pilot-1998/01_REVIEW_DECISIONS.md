# 1998 Pilot Review Decisions

Decision date: 2026-09-08  
Decision authority: project owner  
Status: **retain records with explicit discrepancy states; no canonical promotion**

## What

These decisions resolve how the pilot treats the five review areas identified after coordinated research. They do not resolve the underlying historical unknowns.

## Decisions

### Pentium II OverDrive 300/333 — `L3`

Keep both frozen candidate records and preserve the question of whether they represent one physical product with two output mappings or separate products. Do not merge, replace, or reinterpret them unless verified production/release evidence resolves the conflict. Acceptable future evidence may include attributable testimony from someone involved in production or release, or documentary material that clearly establishes the product identity.

### Pentium II Xeon 450 — `L3`

Keep the record. Its documented specifications and design contribute to the database even while the announcement-versus-commercial-shipment boundary remains open. Review the level if stronger evidence establishes actual shipment, availability, or the intended cohort event.

### Quantum Fireball EL — `L2`

Keep the record as valid. Commercial existence and May 1998 mass production are supported. Exact first commercial release or shipment remains unknown and must be displayed as a possible release discrepancy, not used as a disqualifier.

### GPU forecast-versus-shipment cases

- `intel-i740` — `L2`: February production is stated; exact commercial shipment remains unconfirmed.
- `matrox-mga-g200` — `L3`: the chip announcement is supported; concrete products and release dates were undisclosed.
- `ati-rage-128-gl` — `L4`: September and October production forecasts conflict, and neither establishes actual shipment.
- `ati-rage-128-vr` — `L4`: the same production-forecast conflict remains unresolved for the distinct VR variant.
- `3dfx-voodoo-banshee` — `L2`: announcement is supported; board availability was forecast rather than confirmed.

All remain useful database candidates. The level describes the unresolved evidence boundary.

### Omission leads — `OL`

Retain every omission lead as a deferred research pointer. The current CPU/GPU/HDD sample is sufficiently representative to test and scale the workflow across other years and categories. Granular expansion toward a 1998 census is postponed until it becomes a deliberate research objective.

## Future contribution note

The OverDrive and similar cases are good candidates for a later contribution workflow. A contribution should not directly overwrite a record. It should create a reviewable claim with contributor relationship, documentary evidence or attributable testimony, provenance and permission, the affected field, and an independent acceptance decision.
