# GPU Normalization Notes

Covered exactly eight frozen GPU keys. Chip and board entities remain separate: Intel740 is a chip while Express 3D is a board; Banshee and the ATI/Matrox records remain chip-level proposals. Month precision is preserved for chronology and forecast records.

`review_required` is used for Intel740, Matrox MGA-G200, ATI RAGE 128GL/VR, and 3dfx Voodoo Banshee because planned or forecast availability does not establish a qualifying 1998 shipment, or because production forecasts conflict. NVIDIA chronology entries are proposed at month precision without inheriting board identities.

No candidates were added, no verification claims were changed, and no database or shared batch files were edited.
