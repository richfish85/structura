# GPU Scout 001 — 1998 Launch Cohort

## Scope

This is a bounded 1998 GPU launch-cohort scout: products newly introduced or with a stated first commercial/volume-shipment event during 1998. It includes chip and board identities only where the source makes the distinction explicit. It excludes earlier products merely still on sale, does not normalize names, and makes no verification or canonical decision.

## Candidate coverage

The seven-chip/one-board sample spans Intel, NVIDIA, Matrox, ATI, and 3Dfx; mainstream desktop, workstation/professional, OEM/integrated, and dealer-reseller segments are present where sourced. The Intel Express 3D card is intentionally separate from the Intel740 chip. ATI RAGE 128GL and VR are separately scoped chip candidates because the contemporary source distinguishes their interfaces and uses; no RAGE 128 board is substituted for either chip.

## Evidence quality and gaps

- Intel sources are contemporary first-party releases with clear product and availability language.
- NVIDIA's official timeline supports month-level launches only; exact dates and first commercial shipments remain unknown.
- Matrox, ATI, and 3Dfx records use contemporary technical reporting because the original manufacturer pages are no longer directly available in the working source set. Their linked original-release references are leads, not separate registered evidence.
- ATI sources disagree on the expected volume-production month (September in PC Watch's report, October in Microprocessor Report). Both reports support announcement/sampling, but neither establishes an actual first commercial shipment. The conflict is retained in candidate notes for verification.
- Banshee board availability was expected, not confirmed. It remains a chip candidate.

## Omission seed list — not candidates

These are bounded audit leads, not additions to this scout batch: 3Dfx Voodoo2 (1998 retail/board timing and chip-versus-board identity), S3 Savage3D (announcement versus late-1998 shipment), Number Nine Revolution 3D, Rendition V2200, 3DLabs Permedia 3, and any 1998 mobile GPU product with a source establishing an actual product introduction or shipment. Later verification should also check board products built around RIVA TNT, MGA-G200, and RAGE 128 without conflating them with the chips.

## Stopping rationale

The pass stops after representative manufacturer and segment coverage plus explicit chip/board separation. It does not claim a complete 1998 GPU census: source survival for several manufacturers is uneven, and the omission seed list identifies the next bounded audit work.
