# GPU Verification Notes — Frozen 1998 Scout

## Scope

This verifier run covers exactly the eight frozen scout keys. It does not add candidates, normalize names, audit the cohort, or promote canonical records. Source use is restricted to the seven URLs registered in the frozen scout sources.csv.

## Verification conclusions

- Intel740 is verified as a chip announcement with February 1998 volume-production wording; no Intel740 board is inferred.
- Intel Express 3D is verified as a distinct Intel-branded add-in graphics card with immediate dealer/distributor/system-integrator availability on 1998-04-15.
- NVIDIA RIVA 128ZX and RIVA TNT are verified as chip launches from NVIDIA's first-party chronology only. Preserve February and March month precision; no exact day, board, or shipment claim is made.
- Matrox MGA-G200 is verified as a 1998-05-01 chip announcement, but the registered contemporary source explicitly says concrete products and release dates were not disclosed. It remains review-required for the cohort event rule.
- ATI RAGE 128GL and RAGE 128VR are verified as distinct chip/package variants. PC Watch reports September planned volume production; Microprocessor Report reports October planned volume production. Both sources describe plans/sampling, not confirmed first commercial shipment. The conflict is retained.
- 3Dfx Voodoo Banshee is verified as a chip announcement on 1998-06-22. The source forecasts Banshee-card availability in August or September; that forecast is not treated as shipment evidence.

## Chip versus board boundary

Intel740, RAGE 128GL, RAGE 128VR, RIVA 128ZX, RIVA TNT, and Voodoo Banshee remain chip candidates. Intel Express 3D remains a board candidate. ATI board names mentioned in the registered source (RAGE MAGNUM, RAGE FURY, XPERT 128) are not added or substituted.
