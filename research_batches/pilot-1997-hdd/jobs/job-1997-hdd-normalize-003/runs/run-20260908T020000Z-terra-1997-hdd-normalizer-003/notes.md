# 1997 HDD normalization notes

## What

Five frozen scout-and-verifier keys were converted into provisional family-level HDD normalization proposals.

## Why

The record set needs consistent names and explicitly bounded qualifying events before human review, especially where product announcements, sample supply, production, OEM availability, and retail availability differ.

## How

- All five records remain families; no capacity/interface/model variants were invented or split.
- Fireball SE's mass production is not treated as commercial shipment.
- Deskstar 8 remains OEM-only; DiamondMax 1750's retail context remains anticipated rather than confirmed.
- Bigfoot TX remains limited by retrospective evidence.
- Deskstar 18GP remains OEM-only, non-retail, with system availability reported after the 1997 COMDEX event.

All rows are non-canonical and review-required. No scout/verifier artifacts, database records, or shared batch files were changed.
