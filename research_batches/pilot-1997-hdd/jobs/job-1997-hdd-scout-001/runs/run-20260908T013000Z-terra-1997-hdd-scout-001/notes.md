# 1997 HDD scout notes

## Scope

Five representative fixed-magnetic HDD records cover desktop IDE and OEM/enterprise boundaries. They are families/models as published; no capacity or interface variants were split into new candidate entities.

## Timing and channel limits

- Fireball SE and Bigfoot TX have mass-production evidence, not a dated first commercial shipment.
- Deskstar 8 is explicitly OEM-only; its sample and production statements do not establish retail availability.
- DiamondMax 1750 has planned production timing and an anticipated US retail price, not confirmed retail sale.
- Deskstar 18GP was announced/displayed in November but systems using it were expected months later; it is a cohort-boundary review case.

## Omission leads

Separate future scout scopes could examine Quantum Atlas III and Viking II as 1998-production exclusions; Seagate Medalist/Barracuda families; Fujitsu desktop and mobile lines; Western Digital Caviar products; IBM Travelstar mobile families; and removable HDD products. These are not candidates in this run.
