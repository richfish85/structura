# Scout Notes — 1998 HDD Launch Cohort

## Scope

This bounded pilot covers fixed magnetic hard-disk-drive families and model variants introduced or entering first commercial shipment during calendar 1998, limited to desktop/workstation-class products from Quantum and IBM with contemporary dateable evidence. Mobile, removable, enterprise/SCSI, and other manufacturers remain omission-search territory unless separately seeded below. Family records remain families; named model variants remain separate.

## Evidence summary

- Quantum Fireball EL: Quantum's SEC filing states that mass production began in May 1998. The source gives 3.5-inch form factor, Ultra ATA interface, 5,400 RPM, Shock Protection System, and capacities of 2.5/5.1/7.6/10.2 GB. The exact announcement date and first commercial shipment are not stated.
- Quantum Fireball EX: Quantum's SEC filing states June 1998 announcement and July 1998 mass production. Contemporary PC Watch dates the announcement to June 8 and reports July mass-production shipment, 3.2/5.1/6.4/10.2/12.7 GB capacities, IDE-only connection, and OEM sample pricing.
- IBM Deskstar 25GP: IBM News and Reuters reporting identify a November 1998 introduction of a 25 GB, 5,400 RPM Ultra ATA/66 desktop drive. Limited quantities were shipped to PC makers for Christmas 1998; distributor/reseller availability was deferred to Q1 1999.
- IBM Deskstar 22GXP: The same evidence identifies a 22 GB, 7,200 RPM Ultra ATA/66 desktop drive aimed at video editors, engineers, scientists, and other power users. It shared the limited-OEM 1998 shipment / broader-1999 channel timing.

## Omission seed list

These are leads for a later bounded expansion, not candidates in this run:

- IBM Microdrive 170/340 MB: announced in September 1998 but contemporary evidence says production/shipment was planned for mid-1999, so excluded by the first-commercial-shipment rule.
- Quantum high-end SCSI families (Viking II, Atlas III) and Quantum Bigfoot TX: present in 1998 corporate product listings but exact new-introduction dates are not established here.
- Seagate Medalist/Barracuda/Cheetah, Western Digital Expert, Maxtor DiamondMax, Fujitsu, and Hitachi 3.5-inch families: manufacturer/date coverage not completed in this bounded pilot.
- Mobile 2.5-inch HDD families and enterprise/server HDD launches: outside the declared desktop/workstation boundary and require a separate segment pass.

## Gaps and conflicts

- Quantum EL evidence supports May mass production but not an exact introduction or first-shipment day.
- IBM evidence distinguishes limited OEM shipments in 1998 from distributor/reseller availability in Q1 1999; the qualifying event is first limited commercial shipment, not broad retail release.
- IBM source reports use consumer-facing capacity labels; ordering part numbers, regional launch timing, and exact 1998 shipment dates are unresolved.
- All four candidates need independent verification before normalization. No canonical or verification conclusions were written.
