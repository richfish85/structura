# 1997–1998 Phones Pilot — Status

Status: **six provisional candidates staged; independent verification and one final audit complete; blockers corrected; no canonical records**

## Scope

Six representative Nokia models with a 1997 or 1998 introduction/launch claim: 9000i Communicator, 6110 and 8110i (1997); 9110 Communicator, 5110 and 8810 (1998). This is not a market census. All remain noncanonical candidates.

## Era-aware classification

A historical Smartphone proposal requires evidence of an advanced operating environment and application functions. Capacitive multi-touch is not required. The 9000i and 9110 Communicators are proposed as Smartphones; the 6110, 8110i, 5110 and 8810 are proposed as Legacy Phones because reviewed sources do not establish an advanced application environment.

## Event boundaries

Introduction, announcement, retail availability and volume delivery are different events. Nokia reports 6110 announcement in late 1997 and volume deliveries early 1998; Digi reports a 10 Nov press launch while a museum gives 18 Dec. Nokia says 5110 introduced March 1998 and volume delivery May. Nokia says 9110 introduced in 1998 but volume delivery began 1 Feb 1999. These distinctions remain explicit.
