# 1997 CPU Scout Notes

This is a representative launch cohort, not a census. It covers Intel desktop, mobile, embedded, and workstation timing boundaries plus an AMD family-level shipment record. No model numbers or speed-grade membership are inferred where the source does not supply them.

## Explicit omission leads

Future bounded searches should consider Cyrix 6x86MX, IDT WinChip, IBM/Cyrix 6x86 variants, additional AMD-K6 speed-grade/package records, Intel Pentium II server/workstation configurations, Intel OverDrive variants, and other 1997 x86 introductions outside the selected Intel/AMD slice.

## Unknowns and limits

Exact first-shipment dates, regional availability, ordering part numbers, and boxed/OEM distinctions remain incomplete. AMD-K6 is intentionally family-level; its 166/200/233 MHz launch composition is not asserted here. Intel chronology is corroboration, not a substitute for channel-specific shipment evidence.

No verification, normalization, audit, context research, database write, or canonical promotion was performed.
